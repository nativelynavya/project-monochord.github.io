Chapter 2: Supply, Demand & Market Equilibrium

Explore how changes in supply and demand affect the price and quantity traded. Move the controls to hear the market move away from balance: the two tones beat faster as the imbalance grows.

Market controls

A positive supply shift means more supply and can create a surplus. A positive demand shift means stronger demand and can create a shortage. Use the sliders' arrow keys or enter a value from -50 to 50.

Supply and demand curves
