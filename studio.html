Monochord Educator Studio: Custom Graph Sonifier

Make a graph lesson your students can explore visually and by sound. Higher Y-values map to higher musical pitches.

Create a custom graph

Enter at least two comma-separated finite numbers (up to 40 values). Each value is plotted in order at X positions 1, 2, 3, and so on.

Values are read left to right and mapped from 220 Hz (lowest value) to 880 Hz (highest value).

Explore the curve

Focus the graph and use the Left and Right Arrow keys to move between points. Each point's value and pitch are announced and played.
