Currency Appreciation & Depreciation

Explore a sample of the USD to INR exchange rate over six months. The rate is Indian rupees per one US dollar: when the number rises, the US dollar appreciates against the rupee; when it falls, it depreciates. Each month is a note, with higher rates mapped to higher pitches.

Explore the six-month trend

The sample values are illustrative, not live financial data. Use the chart or the exact-value table to explore each month.

Choose a preset or edit the exchange rate for any month. Values are INR per one USD. Each change updates the chart and previews that month's note.

Monthly exchange rates

January is the starting exchange rate. Changing it shifts the other months by the same amount, preserving their trend. The number field and slider for each month stay in sync.
